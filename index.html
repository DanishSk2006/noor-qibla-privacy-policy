2. Application Features & Data Interaction

NoorQibla is a comprehensive Islamic utility application engineered with privacy-by-design. Below is a detailed description of all app features and their respective data interactions:

⏰

1. Accurate Prayer Times & Solar Milestones

Calculates Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, Qiyam, Islamic Midnight, and Last Third of Night using astronomical sun position algorithms on device. Supports multiple worldwide calculation conventions (MWL, ISNA, Egypt, Makkah, Karachi, Tehran, Jafari) and Hanafi/Shafi'i Asr juristic methods.

🔊

2. Automatic Azan Audio & Namaz Alarms

Plays the call to prayer (Azan) or Takbeer automatically when Namaz time arrives. Includes high-priority lock-screen and closed-app alert popups with immediate "Stop" and "After 2 min" snooze buttons, custom audio file support, and unattended auto-stop safeguards.

🧭

3. High-Precision Qibla Compass

Uses your device's built-in magnetometer and accelerometer to compute real-time bearing toward the Holy Kaaba in Makkah al-Mukarramah. Provides visual alignment rings and haptic vibration feedback. Calculations run entirely in local memory.

🕌

4. Nearby Masjids Finder

Identifies mosques in your vicinity using mathematical spherical distance coordinates. Enables searching by masjid name or neighborhood and opens external map navigation only upon explicit user tap.

📖

5. Holy Quran Reader

Features complete Quranic Surahs with Arabic calligraphy text, authentic English translations, and Ayah-by-Ayah reading. Allows bookmarking favorite verses stored locally in the offline database.

🤲

6. Authentic Islamic Duas & Athkar

Comprehensive repository of Hisnul Muslim supplications categorized for morning, evening, travel, protection, forgiveness, and Salah. Includes Arabic text, transliteration, English meanings, and one-tap favorite marking.

📿

7. Digital Smart Tasbih Counter

Tactile Dhikr counter with customizable targets (33, 99, 100, custom), haptic vibration feedback on completion, sound effects, reset safety confirmation, and persistent tally retention.

📅

8. Month-Wise Islamic Calendar & Timetable

Complete monthly Salah timetable with day-by-day lookup, dual Gregorian and Hijri calendar dates, and Imsak/Fasting schedules. Accessible completely offline.

🌙

9. Ramadan Fasting & Ibadah Tracker

Live Suhoor and Iftar countdown timers, daily Ramadan checklist (Fasting, Tarawih, Quran recitation, Sadaqah charity), and persistent historical logs to track spiritual consistency.

📋

10. Detailed Namaz Rakat Guide

Comprehensive educational guide specifying exact rakat counts (Farz, Sunnah Mu'akkadah, Sunnah Ghair Mu'akkadah, Nafl, Witr) for Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, Jummah, Tahajjud, and Taraweeh prayers.

3. Android Permissions & Hardware Usage

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies, NoorQibla only requests permissions that are strictly necessary to deliver its core religious utility features. Below is our complete, transparent permissions disclosure:

Permission Type Purpose & Privacy Safeguard
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION		 Location Purpose: Needed exclusively to calculate accurate local prayer times (based on solar zenith angles), determine the exact compass bearing to the Kaaba in Makkah, and calculate distances to nearby masjids.
Privacy Safeguard: Coordinates are processed locally on your phone. Your location is NEVER transmitted to remote servers, never logged, and never shared with advertisers or third parties.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM
USE_EXACT_ALARM		 Alarm Purpose: Allows the app to wake the system at the exact second prayer time starts, even in Android battery Doze mode, so you never miss Salah.
Privacy Safeguard: Used strictly for user-configured Namaz alarms. Contains no background analytics.
USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT Alarm Popup Purpose: Enables displaying the high-priority Azan alarm popup over the lock screen or while the app is closed, presenting immediate "Stop" and "After 2 min" snooze buttons.
Privacy Safeguard: Only displayed when an enabled prayer alarm triggers.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Notification Purpose: Displays upcoming prayer countdowns, Azan alerts, and interactive notification buttons ("Stop", "After 2 min") on Android 13+.
Privacy Safeguard: No promotional or commercial push notifications are ever sent.
VIBRATE Hardware Purpose: Provides haptic feedback when the Qibla compass aligns with Makkah, gives tactile pulses on Tasbih counts, and supports silent prayer alerts.
WAKE_LOCK Audio Purpose: Ensures Azan audio playback plays uninterrupted when the screen turns off during prayer time.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED System Purpose: Automatically reschedules prayer alarms when your phone restarts, ensuring alarm continuity.
INTERNET Network Purpose: Used for reverse-geocoding city names when detecting location, and accessing external map links when navigating to a masjid.

4. Local Data Storage & Retention

All personal customizations and activity records in NoorQibla are stored locally in an on-device SQLite database utilizing the Android Room persistence library. This includes:

  • Prayer Preferences: Selected calculation methods, juristic rules, alarm sound choices, volume, and offset minutes.
  • Saved Locations: Cached coordinates and city names for offline prayer time calculations.
  • Spiritual Records: Quran bookmarks, favorite Duas, Tasbih count histories, and Ramadan fasting logs.

Data Retention & Deletion: You have absolute control over this data. Since data is stored solely on your physical device, clearing the app's cache/storage through Android Settings or uninstalling NoorQibla permanently removes all stored information immediately.
